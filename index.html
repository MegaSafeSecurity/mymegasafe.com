01 Director’s message

Director’s Message

Welcome to the official website of Mega Safe Security Services Sdn Bhd.

Mega Safe Security Services Sdn Bhd is a reputable commercial security company established in Malaysia under the Private Agencies Act 1971. With a strong commitment to excellence and professionalism, we have built a reputation as a trusted and reliable security service provider.

We are committed to meeting the diverse security needs of our valued clients. Through careful planning, modern technology, and a highly skilled workforce, we provide a comprehensive range of services to protect assets, premises, and our clients’ peace of mind.

As demand for quality security solutions continues to grow, we remain ready to meet challenges with proven expertise and unwavering dedication. Our trained and experienced personnel uphold integrity and discipline to ensure reliable and effective service delivery.

Driven by a commitment to excellent service and protecting what matters most, Mega Safe Security Services Sdn Bhd continues to build long-term partnerships based on trust, performance, and professionalism.

07 Our Services

Our Services

Security solutions built
around your environment.

Mega Safe delivers tailored security solutions for specific client threat profiles and operational environments.

01

Commercial & Industrial Guarding

  • Static Guards — Well-trained, uniform personnel for physical deterrence, entry-point defence and facility vigilance.
  • Access Control & Visitor Management — Rigorous logging, credential verification and structured management of personnel, contractors and guests.
  • Perimeter Security — Systematic mobile and foot patrols to secure boundaries, identify vulnerabilities and prevent intrusion.
↗
02

Event Security & Crowd Control

  • Corporate & Public Events — End-to-end event risk management, crowd safety enforcement and access monitoring.
  • VIP Escort & Protection Control — Dedicated specialised teams for personal protection, movement security and discreet crowd management.

03

Security Risk Assessment & SOP Planning

  • Premise Risk Evaluation — Comprehensive on-site audits to identify physical, structural and operational security gaps.
