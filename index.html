02 Company Information

Corporate parameter

Trusted Security Solutions Built on Integrity and Discipline

Mega Safe Security Services Sdn Bhd is a licensed commercial security provider based in Sabah, Malaysia, operating under the Private Agencies Act 1971. We deliver tailored, reliable and modern security solutions designed to protect your people, premises and assets. Backed by proven industry experience and a well-trained workforce, we give businesses and communities lasting peace of mind—24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
