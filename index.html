Director’s Message

Welcome to the official website of Mega Safe Security Services Sdn Bhd.

It is my pleasure to welcome you to Mega Safe Security Services Sdn Bhd. Established under the Private Agencies Act 1971, we are a licensed commercial security provider committed to professionalism, integrity and dependable service.

We understand that every client has different security needs. Through careful planning, modern security practices and a well-trained workforce, we protect assets, premises and people, and give our clients lasting peace of mind.

03 Introduction

Built on
professionalism
and reliable service.

Incorporated in 2003 under the Private Agencies Act 1971, Mega Safe Security Services Sdn Bhd has built more than two decades of experience in providing comprehensive physical security solutions across Malaysia.

Our work is founded on three principles: Discipline, Responsibility and Punctuality. These principles have earned us a reputation for high standards and operational reliability in protecting commercial, industrial and public assets.

We combine strategic planning, modern security technology and a rigorously trained workforce, from routine facility access control to complex risk mitigation, to deliver structured vigilance and professional execution.

06 Our Services

Our Services

Security solutions built
around your environment.

We deliver security solutions tailored to each client’s threat profile and operating environment.

01

Commercial & Industrial Guarding

  • Static Guards — Well-trained, uniformed personnel providing physical deterrence, entry-point control and facility vigilance.
  • Access Control & Visitor Management — Systematic logging, credential verification and structured management of staff, contractors and visitors.
  • Perimeter Security — Scheduled mobile and foot patrols that secure boundaries, identify vulnerabilities and prevent intrusion.
